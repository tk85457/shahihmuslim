## 🌟 About Sahih Muslim

**Sahih Muslim (صحيح مسلم)**, compiled by **Imam Muslim ibn al-Hajjaj (rahimahullah)**, is the second most authentic collection of Hadith in Islamic literature after Sahih Al-Bukhari.

This Flutter application delivers the complete **54 Books and 7,500+ authentic Hadiths** with high-speed search, side-by-side Urdu & English translations, Arabic text, and dark mode support.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📜 **Complete Collection**: All 54 Books & 7,500+ Authentic Hadiths of Sahih Muslim.
- 🌐 **Dual Translations**: Clear side-by-side **Urdu** (صحيح مسلم اردو) and **English** translations.
- ⚡ **Instant Search Engine**: Sub-50ms search by Hadith number, Arabic roots, English keywords, or Urdu words.
- 📡 **100% Offline**: Embedded database requiring no internet connection.
- 📌 **Bookmarks & Research Notes**: Organize favorite Hadiths and save personal research notes.
- 🎨 **Islamic Aesthetic UI**: Customizable fonts (Arabic Naskh, Uthmani, Urdu Nastaliq) with dark mode.
